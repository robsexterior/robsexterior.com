Soft Washing in Woodstock, IL

If your home's siding looks dingy, streaked, or green — it's not dirt. It's living organisms. Mold, mildew, algae, and bacteria colonize your siding and gradually break it down. Standard pressure washing blasts the surface but leaves the roots alive, meaning it comes back within months. It can also force water behind your panels, crack caulk, and strip paint.

Soft washing solves the problem properly. Using low water pressure and professional-grade biodegradable cleaning solutions, we kill mold and algae at the root and rinse it away safely — results that last 4–6 times longer than pressure washing. It's the method recommended by most siding manufacturers, and it's what Rob uses on every house wash in Woodstock, IL.

  • Safe for vinyl, wood, stucco, brick, Hardie board, and fiber cement siding
  • Kills mold, mildew, and algae at the root — not just surface cleaning
  • Removes black streaks, green staining, oxidation, and spider webs
  • Results last 4–6x longer than pressure washing alone
  • No damage to paint, caulk, window seals, or plants
  • Biodegradable solutions — safe for your lawn, garden, and pets
